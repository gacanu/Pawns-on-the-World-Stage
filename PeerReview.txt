Code Critique:
The interfaces given were overall well designed, and aside from a few odd design decisions (like using a map, integers to integers, representing the score),
most of the designs were capable of working within many given views, controllers, etc. The only major issue I could find was that the model
handled selected cards and hand indices, as this is ties the model to visual representation and idea of a GUI. The selected cards,
in my opinion, are a detail of player interaction and do not describe the state of the game, and should be kept within the controller and view rather
than the model. Although I couldn't get it fully working, their view was well made and visually represented the game state simply and effectively.
We needed to reach out to the providers once to receive a more updated version of their code (to hopefully fix the selection bug), although it
turned out to be a deeper-running problem that couldn't be solved by simply importing some updated code.
